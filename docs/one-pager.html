What are the steps to lock out a machine before servicing?
Safety procedures Confidence: high · 91%

Before servicing, apply lockout/tagout (LOTO):

  1. Notify affected employees and shut the machine down.
  2. Isolate every energy source and apply your lock and tag.
  3. Release stored energy, then verify a zero-energy state.
Sources
1 OSHA 3120 — Control of Hazardous Energy — Applying Lockout/Tagout Devices · p. 12
2 29 CFR 1910.147 — (d) Application of control
